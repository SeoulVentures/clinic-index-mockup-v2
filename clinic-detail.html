Day 0 — Consultation
English consultation with Dr. Han. Skin assessment and a written estimate. No treatment on the same day unless decided.
Day 1 — Treatment
The procedure itself takes 20–40 minutes. Local anaesthetic for filler and laser.
Day 2–3 — Recovery
Mild swelling and redness. The clinic asks for a photo check on day 2 by message.
Day 7 — Departure
For booster and laser the clinic advises departing on day 7 or later so swelling has settled.

Practical notes for visiting patients

Things we confirmed on the call, which are not on the price list.

  • Deposits for laser packages are paid at the clinic; card and cash are both accepted.
  • The closest station is Exit 4 of Gangnam Station, roughly 6 minutes on foot.
  • For a companion, the clinic can issue a separate visitor pass for each guest.
  • Recovery photos are sent over the messaging channel the clinic uses for consultations.

Common questions

Asked by visiting patients most often.

Can I be treated on the day I arrive?+
Yes for booster and Botox, but the clinic recommends a separate consultation day for laser and filler so swelling is not an issue before a flight.
Is the price the same for everyone?+
The price list is the base rate. Cost per unit for Botox and the amount of filler depend on the area and are decided after the consultation.
Do you accept foreign cards?+
International cards are accepted at the clinic. A 10% promotion on packages was valid in September 2026 and may have changed since.
