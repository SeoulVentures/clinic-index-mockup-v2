How this directory works

We do not buy listings. A clinic appears here because its record came from the government registry and a person checked the details that actually matter for a treatment decision.

1

Registry record

Name, address, coordinates, treatment scope and opening date come from the national medical facility dataset.

2

Manual check

Price list, doctor, opening hours, foreign-language support and a working contact channel are confirmed by a person, on the phone where needed.

3

Published gradually

About 20 clinics a day go live after a check. Verified clinics are indexed by search engines; the rest stay out of the index until verified.

i
Why the count moves slowly

Publishing 2,387 records on day one would give a large list and thin detail. We would rather show a smaller list that is right. Coverage is shown honestly on every page.

!
Not every listing can take your enquiry

Where a clinic has not completed verification, the button says Ask about this area and the request is routed to a verified clinic nearby. We do not pass an enquiry to a clinic we could not reach.

What we cannot tell you

Honest limits, stated up front.

  • Patient reviews and before/after photos are not included in this version.
  • Booking and payment are not handled here — this site only introduces you.
  • English-speaking status is confirmed by phone call, not by the presence of an English menu.
  • Final price is always confirmed at consultation.
