What the treatment involves

The same sequence at every clinic we have checked, with the numbers each clinic gave us.

Consultation — 20 to 30 minutes
The doctor assesses skin condition, checks for contraindications and marks the injection grid. Photographs are taken for the before record.
Application — 20 to 40 minutes
A grid of injections across the face. Topical anaesthetic is applied first. The product is 2cc Rejuran; clinics in Seoul also offer a combined menu that adds a water-glow booster and a skin botox.
Recovery — 1 to 2 days
Small blebs at each injection site, then redness and swelling. It settles within a day. Makeup can be reapplied the next day.
Results — 2 to 4 weeks
Hydration and texture change over several weeks. Clinics commonly recommend a course of three, spaced two to four weeks apart.

Common questions

Is Rejuran the same as a water-glow booster?+
No, though clinics frequently combine them in one package. Rejuran is the salmon DNA product at 2cc. Some packages add a water-glow booster and a small amount of skin botox for a different effect, which is why the price varies so much between clinics. Ask what is in the package before you book.
How many sessions do I need?+
Clinics generally recommend three, spaced two to four weeks apart, then a maintenance session every three to six months. Individual advice depends on your skin and what you are treating.
Can I fly home the next day?+
Swelling usually settles within a day or two, but clinics advise waiting seven days before a long-haul flight. That gives time to check for any reaction and to see the result before you leave.
Does it hurt?+
Topical anaesthetic is used. Patients describe it as uncomfortable rather than painful, and the whole session takes 20 to 40 minutes depending on the area treated.
