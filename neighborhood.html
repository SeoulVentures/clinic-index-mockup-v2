Cheongdam for a first visit

Practical notes from clinics we have checked in this neighborhood.

◷

Most clinics close on Sunday

Saturday hours are shorter, often ending at 15:00. Plan a consultation on a weekday and the treatment on the following day.

↔

Cheongdam Station, Exit 1

Teheran-ro is a ten-minute walk from Cheongdam Station. Several clinics are inside buildings with a shared reception, so check the floor before you go up.

◐

English is not always on the website

A few clinics have an English menu but no English service. We confirm by phone. If a clinic is marked "English OK" here, a person heard it confirmed.
